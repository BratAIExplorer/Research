## If-then rules (my suggestion, simple on purpose)

| If | Then |
|---|---|
| Hike is priced (90%+) and Fed hikes with calm tone | Not a surprise. Continue the staged buying plan. |
| Fed hikes AND signals more hikes, yields jump | PAUSE new buying. Wait for the next checkpoint. |
| Fed does NOT hike when 85%+ expected | Surprise. Wait 1-2 days. Do not chase the pop. |
| Hot inflation + strong jobs | Raise caution. Smaller slice. |
| Earnings estimates get cut | "Cheap" P/E is not cheap. Pause. |
| Big one-day swing (5%+) either way | Do nothing that day. Re-check the list tomorrow. |
